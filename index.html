About Us

We provide a peaceful and supportive environment for elders with complete care, nutritious food, medical assistance, physiotherapy, and emotional support. Now serving at two branches: Chaitanyapuri near Jain Mandir and Saroornagar near Priyadarshini Park.

Our Services

Nutritious Food

Breakfast, lunch, dinner with fresh and homely cooking.

Nursing & Medical Care

Doctor visits, daily monitoring & emergency support.

AC Premium Care

Air-conditioned rooms for comfortable living.

Physiotherapy

Professional physiotherapy for elderly residents.

Support & Donations

You can support us by donating groceries, rice, food items or sponsoring an elderly person. Your contribution brings hope and comfort to their lives.
