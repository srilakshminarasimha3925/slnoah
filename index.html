Why Choose Us?

⭐ 24/7 Support

At Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Old Age Home, our purpose is simple — to give every elder a life filled with love, dignity, comfort, and peace. We believe that elders deserve not just care, but a life that feels like home. That is why we focus on every small detail — from their health, to their comfort, to their happiness.


⭐ Daily Food & Nutrition — Just Like Home

At Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Old Age Home, we provide fresh, hygienic, and homely food every single day. Breakfast: One healthy breakfast item prepared fresh each morning. Lunch: Rice, curry, chutney, sambar or rasam or equivalent, and curd. Dinner: Chapathi, rice, curry, and curd. We understand that food is not just nutrition — it is comfort, it is emotion, and it is what makes elders feel at home. So we make sure every meal is prepared with care, taste, and love.


⭐ Medical Care

Doctor visits, medicines, and regular health checkups.

Because anyone can provide services… but only a few provide care from the heart. And we proudly say… we are one of those few. We don’t just run an old age home — we build a family for the people who feel lonely or need support.

About Us

Sri Lakshmi Narasimha Old Age Home is dedicated to providing a safe and comfortable living environment for elders. Our mission is to care for your loved ones the way you would — with affection, patience, and dignity. With professional caregivers, hygienic facilities, nutritious meals, doctor consultations, physiotherapy support, and 24×7 monitoring, we ensure a peaceful life for our residents. We also offer premium AC rooms and a family-like atmosphere where every elder is treated with love and respect.
